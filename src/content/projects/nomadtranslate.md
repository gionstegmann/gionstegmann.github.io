Nomad Translate was conceived during my [100-day motorcycle journey](/projects/mudmudmoto) from Switzerland to Kazakhstan. While traveling through remote areas with limited connectivity, existing offline translation tools often failed to deliver quality translations or a seamless user experience, leading to many missed opportunities for connection.

Built on Google's TranslateGemma model, optimized for edge devices, Nomad Translate brings powerful, offline-first translation capabilities directly to your phone. It ensures that meaningful conversations with locals are always possible, no matter where the road takes you.

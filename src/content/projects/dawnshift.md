DawnShift is an Android application designed to help users gently shift their wake-up times to a desired target.

It is fully open source and built with modern Android development practices (Kotlin, Jetpack Compose).

Check out the [GitHub repo](https://github.com/gionstegmann/DawnShift) for more information.

MudMudMoto is a creative blog documenting the motorcycle adventures I went on with my dad. 

We recently went on a four-month motorcycle expedition from Zurich, Switzerland to the steppes of Kazakhstan. It was an exercise in extreme resilience, cross-cultural navigation, and storytelling.

We traveled through Europe, Turkey, Iran, Afghanistan, Uzbekistan, Tajikistan, Kyrgyzstan, and Kazakhstan, facing everything from 46°C heat in the Iranian desert to snowstorms at 4600m altitude in the Pamir Highway.

We documented the entire journey through photos, videos, and logs, which you can find on our [website](https://www.mudmudmoto.com) and [Instagram](https://instagram.com/mudmud.moto).
